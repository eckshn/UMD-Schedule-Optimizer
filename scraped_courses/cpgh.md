# CPGH - College Park Scholars-Global Public Health

**Source:** https://academiccatalog.umd.edu/undergraduate/approved-courses/cpgh/

**Total Courses:** 3

---

## CPGH100: College Park Scholars: Global Public Health First-Year Colloquium I

**Credits:** 1

**Restrictions:** Students must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Global Public Health (CPGH) program

**Description:**

Introductory colloquium: Examination of factors that determine the health status of populations around the world.

---

## CPGH101: College Park Scholars: Global Public Health First-Year Colloquium II

**Credits:** 1

**Prerequisites:** CPGH100

**Restrictions:** Students must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Global Public Health (CPGH) program

**Description:**

Using case studies, students will examine necessary components to design successful public health interventions.

---

## CPGH200: College Park Scholars: Global Public Health Second-Year Colloquium

**Credits:** 1

**Prerequisites:** CPGH101

**Restrictions:** Students must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Global Public Health (CPGH) program

**Description:**

Building on knowledge and skills gained from the first-year colloquia, students will develop public health interventions that address a public health issue of a particular community.
