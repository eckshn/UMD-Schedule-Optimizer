# CPET - College Park Scholars-Environment, Technology & Economy

**Source:** https://academiccatalog.umd.edu/undergraduate/approved-courses/cpet/

**Total Courses:** 3

## CPET100: College Park Scholars: Environment, Technology & Economy First-Year Colloquium I

**Credits:** 1

**Restrictions:** Students must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Environment, Technology & Economy (CPET) program

**Description:**

Introductory colloquium: Examination of issues related to the convergence of the environment, technology and the economy.

---

## CPET101: College Park Scholars: Environment, Technology & Economy First-Year Colloquium II

**Credits:** 3

**Prerequisites:** CPET100

**Restrictions:** Students must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Environment, Technology & Economy (CPET) program

**Description:**

Introductory colloquium II: Continued examination of issues related to the convergence of the environment, technology and the economy. Group projects in sustainable development.

## CPET200: College Park Scholars: Environment, Technology & Economy Second-Year Colloquium
