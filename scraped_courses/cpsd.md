# CPSD - College Park Scholars-Science, Discovery & the Universe

**Source:** https://academiccatalog.umd.edu/undergraduate/approved-courses/cpsd/

**Total Courses:** 3

---

## CPSD100: College Park Scholars: Science, Discovery & the Universe First-Year Colloquium A

**Credits:** 1

**Restrictions:** Students must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Science, Discovery & the Universe (CPSD) program

**Description:**

Introductory colloquium: Examination of issues related to science, discovery and the universe.

---

## CPSD101: College Park Scholars: Science, Discovery & the Universe First-Year Colloquium B

**Credits:** 1

**Restrictions:** Students must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Science, Discovery & the Universe (CPSD) program

**Description:**

Introductory colloquium: Examination of the intersection of astronomy and culture; and the roles of science, exploration and communication in the process of discovery.

---

## CPSD200: College Park Scholars: Science, Discovery & the Universe Second-Year Colloquium

**Credits:** 1

**Prerequisites:** CPSD100, CPSD101

**Restrictions:** Students must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Science, Discovery & the Universe (CPSD) program

**Description:**

Advanced colloquium: Continued examination of issues related to science, discovery and the universe.
