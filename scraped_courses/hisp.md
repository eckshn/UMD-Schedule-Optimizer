## HISP200: The Everyday and the American Environment

**Credits:** 3

**Description:**

An introduction to the theories of the everyday within the context of the American built environment. Focuses primarily on the American experience of underrepresented, minority, and/or immigrant communities; both historical and contemporary. Attempts to challenge what is meant by American in describing the American everyday built environment.Jointly offered withHISP615.
