# CPBE - College Park Scholars-Business, Society, and Entrepreneurship

**Total Courses:** 5

## CPBE100: College Park Scholars: Business, Society & Entrepreneurship First-Year Colloquium

**Credits:** 1

**Restrictions:** Students must be matriculated into the College Park Scholars Business, Society & Entrepreneurship (CPBE) program

**Description:**

Introductory colloquium: Examination of issues related to business, society and entrepreneurship.

---

## CPBE101: College Park Scholars: Business, Society and Entrepreneurship First-Year Colloquium II

**Credits:** 1

**Prerequisites:** CPBE100

**Restrictions:** Students must be matriculated into the College Park Scholars Business, Society & Entrepreneurship program

**Description:**

The course is the second of three colloquia for the BSE Scholars program scheduled for their second semester (Spring). This colloquium is designed to emphasize the entrepreneurial focus of the BSE program by giving students the opportunity for practical, real-life and personal exposure to entrepreneurial activities.

---

## CPBE200: College Park Scholars: Business, Society and Entrepreneurship: Second-Year Colloquium I

**Credits:** 1

**Prerequisites:** CPBE101

**Restrictions:** Must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Business, Society, & Entrepreneurship (CPBE) program

**Formerly:** CPSP218B

**Description:**

The third of three colloquia for the BSE Scholars program is designed to expose and engage students in real-life and real-time entrepreneurial challenges. BSE partners with two organizations that bring current and relevant challenges to the class. Students work in small teams to explore a highly experiential start-up challenge.

---

## CPBE225: Enterprise Communications

**Credits:** 3

**Prerequisites:** CPBE100

**Restrictions:** Must be enrolled in College Park Scholars' Business, Society and Entrepreneurship (CPBE) program

**Formerly:** CPSP220

**Description:**

Examines basic interpersonal communication processes within written and oral channels, with practical applications for the business environment.

## CPBE270: Impacts of Globalization

**Credits:** 3

**Prerequisites:** CPBE100

**Restrictions:** Students must be matriculated into the College Park Scholars Business, Society & Entrepreneurship (CPBE) program

**Cross-listed with:** HONR298A

**Description:**

Introduces students to key global concepts, discusses a range of perspectives on globalization and cultural competence, and offers an applied learning experience.
