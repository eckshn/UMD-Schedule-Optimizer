# CPCV - College Park Scholars-Civic Engagement for Social Good

**Source:** https://academiccatalog.umd.edu/undergraduate/approved-courses/cpcv/

**Total Courses:** 5

---

## CPCV100: College Park Scholars: Civic Engagement for Social Good First-Year Colloquium I

**Credits:** 1

**Restrictions:** Must be in the College Park Scholars-Civic Engagement for Social Good (CPCV) program

**Formerly:** BSCV181

**Description:**

Introductory colloquium: Examination of issues related to civic engagement.

---

## CPCV101: College Park Scholars: Civic Engagement for Social Good First-Year Colloquium II

**Credits:** 1

**Prerequisites:** CPCV100

**Restrictions:** Must be in the College Park Scholars Civic Engagement for Social Good (CPCV) program

**Formerly:** BSCV182

**Description:**

Examination of domestic societal issues and their national, regional, and local dimensions from political, economic, and policy perspectives. Students will work with local direct service non-profit organizations.

---

## CPCV200: College Park Scholars: Civic Engagement for Social Good Second-Year Colloquium I

**Credits:** 1

**Prerequisites:** CPCV101

**Restrictions:** Must be in the College Park Scholars-Civic Engagement for Social Good (CPCV) program

**Description:**

Through substantive civic engagement work in local communities, in-person classes, and reflective practice, students will develop essential skills to collaborate with organizations and stakeholders in order to create meaningful change in their communities.

## CPCV225: Introduction to Civic Engagement for Social Good

**Credits:** 3

**Restrictions:** Must be in the College Park Scholars-Civic Engagement for Social Good (CPCV) program

**Formerly:** BSCV191

**Description:**

Foundational concepts and principles of community engagement and social change.

---

## CPCV230: College Park Scholars: Civic Engagement for Social Good Internship Practicum

**Credits:** 3

**Restrictions:** Must be in College Park Scholars - Civic Engagement for Social Good (CPCV)

**Formerly:** BSCV302

**Description:**

As the culmination of the CESG experience, students gain hands-on experience in an area related to civic engagement through internships in non-profit organizations, political engagement, community outreach, or research labs. The internship gives students authentic experiences that help them develop hard and soft skills to support their community engagement efforts and their work toward social good.
