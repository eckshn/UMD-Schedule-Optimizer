# QMMS - Quantitative Methodology: Measurement and Statistics

**Source:** https://academiccatalog.umd.edu/undergraduate/approved-courses/qmms/

**Total Courses:** 2

---

## QMMS410: Classroom Assessment

**Credits:** 3

**Restrictions:** Junior standing or higher

**Formerly:** EDMS410

**Description:**

Developing and using classroom assessments, including tests, performance assessments, rating scales, portfolios, observations and oral interactions; basic psychometric statistics; standard setting; grading; communicating assessment information; testing ethics; locating and evaluating measures; program evaluation and classroom research; assessments used for educational policy decisions.

---

## QMMS451: Applied Educational Statistics

**Credits:** 3

**Restrictions:** Sophomore standing or higher

**Formerly:** EDMS451

**Description:**

Introduction to statistical reasoning; location and dispersion measures; data analysis; regression and correlation; formation of hypotheses tests;
