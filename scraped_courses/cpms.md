## CPMS200: College Park Scholars: Media, Self and Society: Second-Year Colloquium I

**Credits:** 1

**Prerequisites:** CPMS101

**Restrictions:** Must be enrolled in the College Park Scholars Media, Self and Society (CPMS) program

**Formerly:** CPSP218J

**Description:**

Builds on the foundational media skills that Media Scholars students learned in their freshman year. Students will continue to analyze and create media, but this semester we will place more emphasis on media in your field and major. Students will develop proficiency in using advanced digital media tools, build their professional online presence, enhance their communication and presentation skills, and analyze the changing media landscape - valuable competencies for success in their chosen fields.

---

## CPMS225: Analyzing Media Practice through Theory

**Credits:** 3

**Prerequisites:** CPMS100

**Restrictions:** Must be in the Scholars Media, Self & Society Program

**Formerly:** CPSP222

**Description:**

Media analysis investigating patterns of ownership, the working of media organizations, patterns of coverage and the nature of audiences.
