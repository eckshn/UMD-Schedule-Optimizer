# UMD Computer Science Major - General Track Requirements

## Lower Level Requirements

### Computer Science Core (16 credits)
- **CMSC131** (4cr) - Object-Oriented Programming I
- **CMSC132** (4cr) - Object-Oriented Programming II  
- **CMSC216** (4cr) - Introduction to Computer Systems
- **CMSC250** (4cr) - Discrete Structures

### Mathematics Foundation (8 credits)
- **MATH140** (4cr) - Calculus I
- **MATH141** (4cr) - Calculus II

## Upper Level Requirements

### Computer Science Core (6 credits)
- **CMSC330** (3cr) - Organization of Programming Languages
- **CMSC351** (3cr) - Algorithms

### 400-Level Courses from Areas (15 credits, 5 courses)
**Must take 5 courses from at least 3 different areas, with max 3 from any single area:**

#### Area 1: Systems
- CMSC411, CMSC412, CMSC414, CMSC416, CMSC417

#### Area 2: Information Processing  
- CMSC420, CMSC421, CMSC422, CMSC423, CMSC424
- CMSC426, CMSC427, CMSC470, CMSC471, CMSC472

#### Area 3: Software Engineering & Programming Languages
- CMSC430, CMSC433, CMSC434, CMSC435, CMSC436, CMSC471

#### Area 4: Theory
- CMSC451, CMSC452, CMSC454, CMSC456, CMSC457, CMSC474

#### Area 5: Numerical Analysis
- CMSC460 or CMSC466 (only one counts for credit)

### CS Upper Level Electives (6 credits, 2 courses)
Additional CMSC courses at 300-400 level

## Supporting Courses

### Mathematics/Statistics (10-11 credits)
- **MATH240** (4cr) - Linear Algebra
- **STAT4XX** (3cr) - Statistics course with prereq MATH141
  - Example: STAT400
  - Cannot be cross-listed with CMSC
- **MATH/STAT XXX** (3-4cr) - Additional Math/Stat with prereq MATH141
  - Cannot be cross-listed with CMSC

### Upper Level Concentration (12 credits)
**12 credit hours of 300-400 level courses in ONE discipline outside CS**

Requirements:
- Must be in single discipline (not CS)
- No CMSC courses or courses cross-listed with CMSC
- Maximum 3 credits from independent study/experiential learning
- Minimum cumulative GPA of 1.7 required
- Can overlap with minor/double major (except Information Science)
- Maximum 6 transfer credits allowed

## General Education Requirements (~39 credits)

### Fundamental Studies
- **FSAW** (3cr) - Academic Writing
- **FSOC** (3cr) - Oral Communication  
- **FSPW** (3cr) - Professional Writing
- **FSMA** - Fulfilled by MATH140

### Distributive Studies - Natural Sciences (7 credits total)
- **DSNL** (4cr) - Natural Sciences Lab (also counts toward DSNS)
- **DSNS** (3cr) - Natural Sciences (total 7cr with DSNL)

### Distributive Studies - Other (18 credits)
- **DSHS** (6cr, 2 courses) - History & Social Sciences
- **DSHU** (6cr, 2 courses) - Humanities
- **DSSP** (6cr, 2 courses) - Scholarship in Practice

### Distributive Studies - Diversity (6 credits)
- **DVUP or DVCC** (6cr, 2 courses) - Diversity

## Total Degree Requirements

- **Minimum Total Credits:** 120
- **Minimum CS Credits:** 54
- **Minimum Upper Level Credits:** 30
- **Minimum GPA:** 2.0 overall
- **Minimum Major GPA:** 2.0 in CS courses

## LEP Benchmarks

### 45-Credit Benchmark
- CMSC131 with C- or better
- CMSC132 with C- or better  
- MATH140 with C- or better
- Minimum 2.0 GPA

### 75-Credit Benchmark
- CMSC330 with C- or better
- CMSC351 with C- or better
- STAT4XX with C- or better
- Minimum 2.0 GPA
